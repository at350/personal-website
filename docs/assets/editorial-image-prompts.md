# Editorial image prompt set

These eleven assets were generated as one art-directed family for Issue No. 01.
They use the site's print palette: bright white paper, near-black ink, and one
signal red. The final files are WebP exports in `public/images/`.

## Shared negative direction

Apply this to every prompt: no legible words, letters, numerals, logos,
watermarks, people, screens, UI, gradients, beige, neon, decorative symbols,
hand-drawn illustration, stock-photo gloss, or default-AI clutter. Keep the
image tactile, spare, editorial, and physically plausible.

## Cover fold

Portrait 3:4 conceptual cover artwork photographed as a precise paper sculpture
on a seamless pure-white tabletop. A single bright-white archival sheet is
folded into an architectural form whose central negative space suggests a
capital A without printing or drawing a letter. One narrow vermilion-red slit
appears inside the fold. Near-black cut-paper shadow underneath, hard daylight
from upper left, restrained long shadow, crisp edges, visible fine paper tooth.
Leave generous clean space on the upper-left and left side for typography; keep
the sculpture in the lower-right two-thirds. Museum-catalog still life, bold
NYT Magazine cover logic, minimal Swiss editorial composition.

Output: `public/images/editorial/cover-fold.webp`

## Architec study

Horizontal 3:2 conceptual editorial still life on bright white: stacked white
foam-board elevation planes form a stepped architectural section, crossed by a
compact near-black building mass. A single translucent signal-red acetate plane
cuts through the model like a heat field. Fine graphite construction lines and
tiny registration ticks stay abstract and illegible. Oblique top-down camera,
hard side light, long precise shadows, tactile paper and matte surfaces,
museum-catalog restraint. No skyline or literal software interface.

Output: `public/images/projects/editorial/architec-study.webp`

## GreenChain study

Horizontal 3:2 conceptual editorial still life on a seamless bright-white set.
Five small near-black industrial nodes, joined by taut black thread, sit on
separate white paper platforms at different heights. One signal-red route
threads through a circular aperture and connects the chain. Sharp overhead
light, controlled shadows, charcoal dust and subtle paper tooth. Spare systems
thinking, not an infographic: no globe, map, leaf, recycling arrows, literal
blockchain cubes, or green color.

Output: `public/images/projects/editorial/greenchain-study.webp`

## Prophis study

Horizontal 3:2 conceptual editorial still life: layers of translucent white
vellum carry abstract near-black timeline marks and narrow cut slots, arranged

two moments across the layers. Precise top-down composition with an oblique

## Vox Vera study

Horizontal 3:2 conceptual editorial still life on white. Several narrow
near-black paper channels carry small white tokens toward a single signal-red
crop frame, suggesting many accounts being distilled into one clear view. The
channels converge with strong diagonals and deliberate negative space. Hard
gallery light, precise cast shadows, subtle paper texture, black/white/red only.
No mouths, speech bubbles, microphones, funnel charts, social icons, or text.

Output: `public/images/projects/editorial/vox-vera-study.webp`

## TerraBlade study

Horizontal 3:2 industrial-design editorial still life. A low slate-gray clay
slab rests on a seamless white set, scored by parallel near-black rails and held
by one broad signal-red grip crossing the center. The object should suggest a
compact controlled-motion tool without depicting an exact product or making a
performance claim. Three-quarter macro camera, bright hard light, long precise
shadow, matte rubber and machined-paper textures, restrained product-study
composition. No screen, logo, numbers, blades, or sci-fi styling.

Output: `public/images/projects/editorial/terrablade-study.webp`

## Paper fiber

Square seamless macro material study of bright white archival uncoated paper.
Extremely subtle short cellulose fibers, compressed pulp, pinprick pores, and
soft micro-shadowing; high-key neutral lighting and nearly white overall value.
Physically plausible enough for a WebGL bump map. No folds, tears, stains,
objects, text, borders, directional spotlight, color cast, or repeating motif.

Output: `public/images/editorial/paper-fiber.webp`

## Index: a letter

Portrait 2:3 editorial still life on a warm-white studio sweep. One folded
sheet of white uncoated paper passes through a precise near-black architectural
frame; a taut signal-red thread connects separated paper planes. Soft raking
daylight, restrained shadows, visible paper fibers, one strong central gesture
that survives a narrow full-height crop. Suggest correspondence, structure,
and a person remaining visible inside a system without depicting a person.

Output: `public/images/editorial/index-letter.webp`

## Index: the profile

Portrait 2:3 editorial still life on warm white. Offset white and near-black
card-stock layers contain rectangular apertures; one small signal-red folded
plane is visible through their alignment. Build a quiet profile-like negative
space only through geometry. Vertical rhythm, asymmetrical balance, tactile
paper edges, delicate layered shadows, and a clear thumbnail-scale silhouette.

Output: `public/images/editorial/index-profile.webp`

## Index: five prototypes

Portrait 2:3 tabletop material study on a seamless warm-white set. Exactly five
distinct small physical prototypes form a disciplined vertical system: folded
paper modules, black card channels, pale structural blocks, and translucent
acrylic planes. A single signal-red route travels through all five. Keep every
study countable, handmade, and bold enough for a narrow crop; avoid screens and
literal products.

## Index: the annotated resume

Portrait 2:3 editorial paper still life. Several immaculate white document
sheets stand in a precise vertical arrangement, carrying only abstract black
rules and bars. One signal-red annotation tab emerges from the margin and
physically lifts the top sheet. Raking studio light reveals paper relief,
registration offsets, edge depth, and a delicate crease shadow. All marks stay
completely illegible.

Output: `public/images/editorial/index-resume.webp`
