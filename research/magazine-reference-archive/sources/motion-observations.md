## Motion grammar for the personal site

1. **Entry:** one short cover assembly or object settle; always provide a route into content.
2. **Scroll:** use scroll to cross editorial chapters, not to make every element drift.
3. **Hover:** preview the next state or reveal metadata; never obscure the item being evaluated.
4. **Cursor:** change an object, camera, or readable state; omit decorative followers.
5. **Transition:** preserve origin and destination through page turns, object settling, or state-preserving rearrangement.
6. **Tempo:** controls respond quickly; typography is measured; the book object moves with weight; feature motion stays rare.
7. **Accessibility:** honor reduced motion, keep focus and navigation independent of animation, and make all essential states readable without hover.
